Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare for Android v0.1.2

Nothing to rebuild: the game code you built for v0.1.1 is used as it is, and your saves and settings are kept.

New
- Map packs and Exo Zombies with the phone alone: Add DLC, Choose a folder, pick the folder copied from your own console; the app copies the packs and builds the updated multiplayer itself (about 6 minutes on a Retroid Pocket Flip 2). No DLC is included or downloaded.
- Add DLC says which Compatibility Packs are missing; until all six are there, Multiplayer stays the disc's version.
- Fixed: a match at Internal render multiplier 2x or 3x was washed out white.
- Combat Readiness Program has its bots.
- Multiplayer: an invitation goes only to the player you pick; a private match stays private.
- Updates are simpler: a rebuild is asked for only when the code really changed, and then runs by itself.
- Fixed: the sound slowly fell behind the picture the longer you played.
- Fixed: a piece of the scene could be missing for a single frame.

Known issues
- Rarely, a sun shadow is missing for a single frame; being fixed.
- Sun shadows set to On flicker in some places; keep them on Auto (the default) or Off.
- Map packs are experimental.
- The first minutes on a fresh install stutter while shaders compile.

Never share the game code you build: it contains the game. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Activision or Microsoft.
