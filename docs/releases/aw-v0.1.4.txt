Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare for Android v0.1.4

Nothing to rebuild: the game code you built for v0.1.3 is used as it is, and your saves and settings are kept.

New
- Fixed: in multiplayer the field of view went back to 65 after every death. It now stays as you set it. The campaign's field of view works as before.
- Fixed, on the bundled Turnip driver: one frame in three could draw a model from out-of-date vertex data, which looks like a weapon or model flashing as stray triangles. We found it while looking into a tester's video of the ASM1 drawn as dark triangles every third frame. His glitch itself could not be reproduced, so this fix may or may not be his: please retest and tell us.

Known issues
- A blurred square after a boost dodge or slide, reported by one tester, could not be reproduced and is not fixed. If you see it, please send a video and your log.
- The automatic settings were measured on two devices only; if yours runs worse, send a performance report.
- Map packs are experimental.
- The first minutes on a fresh install stutter while shaders compile.

Official downloads are only on GitHub Releases. Never share the game code you build: it contains the game. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Activision or Microsoft.
